Privacy Policy

The Last Broadcast | Effective August 12, 2026

Short version: the game does not collect, sell, share, or track personal data.

Data collection

The Last Broadcast does not require an account and does not collect names, email addresses, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, advertising identifiers, analytics, or gameplay telemetry.

Local saves

Game progress, settings, choices, and unlocked endings are stored locally on your device. This information is not sent to us. Removing the app or clearing its storage may remove that progress.

Purchases

The game is sold as a paid download. Apple or Google processes the store purchase under its own privacy policy. The game does not receive or store your payment-card information and contains no in-app purchases in version 1.0.

Internet and third parties

Core gameplay works offline. Version 1.0 contains no advertising, analytics, social login, or third-party purchase SDK.

Children

The game is not designed to collect information from children or from any other player.

Changes

If a future update adds online features or data collection, this policy and the store privacy disclosure will be updated before that feature is released.
