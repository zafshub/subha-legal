Subha

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 September 2026

Subha is designed to be private by default. Dhikr counts and app preferences stay on your device, and the app does not require an account. Free users may voluntarily watch a rewarded ad to preview one premium counter for 30 minutes. Pro does not show ads.

The rewarded-ad sections apply to versions that offer this optional feature, including version 1.0.1. Earlier versions without rewarded ads do not use AdMob to serve ads.

Information Stored On Your Device

Subha may store the following on your device:

  • Dhikr counts
  • Streaks and milestones
  • Display name
  • Language and theme preferences
  • Reading preferences
  • Reminder preferences

This information is stored locally so the app can show your progress and remember your settings.

Sharing

Sharing is optional. If you choose to share a milestone card, Subha opens your device's system share sheet. You decide where to send it.

Photos Permission

Subha asks for photo-library permission only when you choose to save a milestone card as an image. The permission is used to save that image to your photo library.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, Subha schedules local notifications on your device for dhikr reminders. Reminder content is stored and scheduled on the device.

Purchases

Subha Pro purchases are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. Subha does not receive your payment-card details.

RevenueCat is used to validate receipts, restore purchases, and determine whether Pro access is active. RevenueCat may process an anonymous app user identifier, device and operating-system details, store receipt or purchase token, purchase history, entitlement status, and the time the app was last seen. This information is used for app functionality, purchase analytics, fraud prevention, and customer support.

Subha does not send your display name or dhikr history to RevenueCat. RevenueCat automatic advertising-identifier collection and diagnostics are disabled in the app.

Optional Rewarded Ads

Free users may choose to watch a rewarded ad supplied through Google AdMob. An ad is never shown automatically. Completing the ad unlocks only the selected premium counter for 30 minutes on that device. Closing the ad before the reward event does not grant the preview. Subha Pro remains ad-free.

Google may collect and share information needed to request, deliver, secure, and measure ads, including IP address (which may be used to estimate approximate location), device and app information, advertising or device identifiers where permitted, ad interactions, crash logs, and other performance and diagnostic information. These data are used for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Subha does not request GPS or precise-location permission.

Subha uses Google's consent tools where required and asks for iOS tracking permission before using the advertising identifier. If iOS tracking permission is not granted, Subha requests a non-personalized rewarded ad, subject to any other required consent. Non-personalized ads still require some data for delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention. After you use rewarded previews, the app may preload the next ad when the stored consent permits it.

Subha does not send your dhikr counts, dhikr history, display name, reminder choices, or religious reading activity to Google for advertising.

You can use core dhikr and basic counting without choosing an ad or purchasing Pro. Where Google's privacy-options form is required, open Ad privacy choices in Settings to review or change your choices. You can also change iOS tracking permission in the device's settings. If ads cannot be requested because of consent or availability, core counting remains available.

See Google's Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from apps, and RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Analytics And Tracking

Subha does not include a separate behavioral analytics or crash-reporting SDK. RevenueCat processes purchase and entitlement events as described above. Google AdMob processes ad delivery, interaction, performance, and diagnostic events for optional rewarded ads. Personalized advertising or cross-app tracking is used only where the user has provided the required consent and system permission.

If another analytics or crash-reporting service is added later, this policy must be updated before release.

Data Deletion

You can delete locally stored counts and progress from Subha's reset controls or by uninstalling the app. To request deletion of purchase-related data processed through RevenueCat, contact zaxskylab@gmail.com. The request will be handled with RevenueCat using the anonymous app user identifier associated with the purchase.

Apple, Google, and RevenueCat may retain transaction, security, or other records under their policies and legal obligations. For Google advertising data, use the privacy controls and request routes described in Google's Privacy Policy.

Religious Content

Subha includes source-checked dhikr content, but it is not a replacement for qualified religious guidance.
